The design-tree approach and related interviewing guidance in `SKILL.md` were
adapted from Matt Pocock's `grilling` skill, invoked by his `grill-me` skill.
This includes tracking dependent decisions, settling prerequisites before
asking dependent questions, investigating accessible facts, and leaving
decisions with the user. The wording and workflow have been adapted for ADR
brainstorming; this is not an unmodified copy of the upstream skill.
